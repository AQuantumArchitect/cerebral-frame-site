# The Frame

A Frame is a live model of one company.

Underneath it is a ledger: the record of what actually happened — orders in, invoices out, jobs started, jobs late, a vendor reply, a Friday count. That record is the truth and it is yours.

On top of the ledger sits a belief graph: what the business most likely looks like right now, given everything the ledger has seen. Every node in it carries three honest states. **Current** means a fresh record backs it. **Stale** means the last record is old enough that the belief is drifting. **Dark** means nobody has measured it, and the Frame says so instead of showing a zero.

That last part is the whole point. A dashboard shows you numbers. A Frame shows you which numbers to trust, which to check, and which you have been running the company without.

## What it is made of

Your existing systems stay. The Frame reads from them — spreadsheets, your accounting package, job tickets, email where it has to — and never becomes another place to type. The model underneath is one we build and maintain ourselves; the map it draws is in plain English, on one page, printable.

## What it is not

It is not a dashboard. It is not a chatbot. It is not a replacement for the person who knows the floor — it is the thing that lets that person stop being the only copy of the truth.
