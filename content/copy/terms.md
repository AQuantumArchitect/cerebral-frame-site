# Terms

This site is an introduction, not a contract. Prices on the work page are typical ranges, not quotes.

Every engagement starts with a $400 half-day consultation. From it we write a proposal: the scope, the price, the deliverables, and what “done” looks like, before anything else starts.

Payment is in thirds against that proposal: one third on signing, one third on delivery, one third on acceptance — you say when it is done, against the deliverables we wrote down.
