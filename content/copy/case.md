# Our own operation, live

This is the Frame of our own company. It is drawn from the machine that runs our operation — the log at the other end of the footer link — and read here as it is. The renderer on this page may reshape a tile; it may never promote one. If the machine says stale, you see stale.

## Why it mostly reads dark or stale today

Because that is what a Frame is for. The machine's last full report is weeks old, so most of what it believed has drifted past its own threshold and says so. The tiles nobody has fed are dark — not zero, not a dash, dark. We could have hidden that behind a screenshot from a good day. We would rather you see the instrument working on us before it works on you.

## What yours would look like instead

Your numbers, drawn from your sources — the accounting export, the job board, the Friday count — against thresholds you set. Current where the record is fresh. Stale where it is not, and visibly. Dark where nobody is measuring yet, which is where the first Build goes. No client Frame has shipped yet; the first one gets our full attention and a written case study, with the client's permission. The door is the [half-day consultation](/work#consultation).
