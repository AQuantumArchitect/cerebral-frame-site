# About

Cerebral Frame is run by Luke Spooner — physicist by training, years in manufacturing and technical sales, builder of systems ever since. The outfit is small and fast by design: one lead, a short bench, no account managers.

We build code and automation for small and mid-size companies, and we build Frames — live models of a business that know what they do not know. The same engine that draws a Frame of a company runs a house, a game, and a research program; that longer story lives on the expedition log linked below.

Remote by default, anywhere. On site when the problem is on the floor.

The long story, and the live Frame of our own operation, are on [the log](https://the-broadcast-production.up.railway.app/); the Frame is also read live on [this site](/case).

## Three years, in brief

Physicist by training, then years in manufacturing and technical sales — the floor, the quote, the customer who needed it Tuesday. Since 2024 the work has been one idea built in several bodies: a model of an operation that knows what it does not know. First a brain for a real house, which has now run for a year on its own records. Then a farming game with a working simulator underneath it, and a small web game that shipped. Then a semantic engine — the part that reads a messy record and decides what it most likely means. Now Frames for companies: the same engine, pointed at a ledger of orders, cash, and late jobs. The longer story, with dates and dead ends, is on [the log](https://the-broadcast-production.up.railway.app/#/story).
